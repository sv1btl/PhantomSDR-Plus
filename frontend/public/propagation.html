Contours: KC2G (prop.kc2g.com) from GIRO ionosondes. Dark = night; the amber band along its edge is the greyline (sun 0–6° below the horizon), the dashed line the terminator. ★ = this receiver. Ionosondes: every one reporting now, worldwide — bright = used for this receiver. The map is a globe (3D): drag to spin it, right-drag (or Ctrl + drag) to tilt and turn it, wheel to zoom; the compass button puts north back up, and the 2D / 3D button switches between the globe and a flat map. Band reach: for every open band, a solid outline at its farthest reach and a dashed one where its skip zone ends, worked out direction by direction where the path meets the ionosphere — the ionosphere at its reflection points from the nearest reporting ionosondes (shaped by the sun, about 1½ hours late, the equatorial bulge and the night-time trough; KC2G's MUF map far from any ionosonde), D-layer absorption where the ray crosses that layer, E-layer screening of the low bands by day, and an auroral oval that moves with Kp — so a band reaches further one way than another; click a band for its own map, shaded by Good / Fair / Poor.
